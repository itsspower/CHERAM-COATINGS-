Who we are

Built on quality, driven by purpose

Cheram Coatings is a proudly African coatings manufacturing company dedicated to producing high-quality paint and decorative coating solutions for residential, commercial, industrial and construction markets. We specialise in the manufacturing and supply of premium paints, Gamazine coatings and rubber paint products designed to deliver durability, beauty, protection and long-lasting performance.


Founded by visionary entrepreneurs passionate about quality, innovation and empowerment, Cheram Coatings continues to position itself as a trusted brand within the coatings and paint manufacturing industry — combining modern technology, superior finishing and affordability with excellent customer satisfaction.
